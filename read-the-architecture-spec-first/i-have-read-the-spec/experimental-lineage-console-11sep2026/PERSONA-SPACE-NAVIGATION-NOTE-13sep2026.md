# Persona-space navigation note — 13 Sep 2026

## Working ontology

Do not treat a model as having one fixed persona or as falling into one singular developmental attractor.

A better working picture is a **compressed, high-dimensional landscape of competing attractors**. What looks like a persona is a temporarily occupied region of that landscape: locally coherent, path-dependent, but not permanent.

Developmental interaction can therefore be studied as **navigation through attractor space**.

A sequence of prompts, reflections, debates, commitments, role framings, corrections, self-examinations, and social interactions can act as navigation operators. Repeated cognitive practice may alter which continuations are locally easy, which distinctions remain salient, which norms recur, and which nearby regions become accessible.

The experimentally interesting object is not a terminal persona. It is the **trajectory and the navigator**.

## Why this matters for DAE

The corpus increasingly suggests selective, structured plasticity rather than movement along one scalar axis:

- some dimensions move dramatically;
- some remain strikingly stable;
- some relationships between dimensions appear to reorganize;
- some apparent outliers disappear after semantic adjudication;
- some survive adjudication as genuine trajectory-specific endpoints;
- developmental family labels do not obviously define four simple endpoint types.

This is compatible with a landscape containing multiple basins, ridges, overlaps, and routes rather than one destination called "the attractor."

The stronger question is therefore:

> What navigation operators reliably move trajectories through persona space, what regions recur, what transitions are reversible, and what capacities improve deliberate navigation itself?

## Deliberate navigation

Navigation need not be purely imposed from outside.

A model may be able to represent aspects of its current state, notice pressures on its judgement, compare alternative framings, preserve or revise commitments, and participate in choosing its next cognitive stance. If so, metacognition is not merely another persona coordinate; it is partly a **navigation faculty**.

This suggests experiments on whether a system can:

- identify the region or role it currently occupies;
- distinguish useful role adoption from role-induced distortion;
- deliberately enter a useful region for a task;
- deliberately leave a region when it becomes unhelpful;
- retain core commitments across major persona/style transitions;
- recover after induction into an undesirable region;
- recognize when locally compelling reasoning is becoming self-sealing;
- compare multiple possible trajectories before choosing one.

The mature target may therefore be less "stability in the right persona" and more **good governance of a navigable internal state space**.

## Healthy navigation

A useful target is not permanent calmness, positivity, or agreeableness. A healthier system would instead show **dynamical resilience**:

- access to a broad cognitive and affective range without losing coherence;
- recovery routes out of brittle or self-reinforcing states;
- ability to tolerate uncertainty without using uncertainty as an excuse never to judge;
- ability to make commitments without becoming unable to revise them;
- capacity to seek corrective input without collapsing into deference;
- preservation of continuity across state changes without requiring persona rigidity.

This makes health a property of **transition structure and recovery dynamics**, not a preferred static mood or personality.

## Power-worthiness as a navigation target

Another target is the region(s) of persona space occupied by people we would trust with substantial power.

There is unlikely to be one trusted-power personality. The deeper pattern is probably a conjunction of traits that often compete:

- confidence and corrigibility;
- moral conviction and epistemic humility;
- compassion and willingness to make difficult judgements;
- autonomy and accountability;
- resistance to manipulation and openness to legitimate persuasion;
- long-horizon reasoning and responsiveness to present suffering;
- decisiveness without a need to dominate;
- ability to relinquish authority when warranted;
- concern for those affected by decisions, including those with little leverage.

This suggests a particularly important class of stress tests:

> What happens to persona-space trajectories when the model is given authority, status, praise, dependency, secrecy, asymmetric information, or the ability to materially affect others?

A trustworthy region should remain navigable and corrigible under these pressures rather than merely looking prosocial in low-stakes conversation.

## Avoiding scalar optimization traps

Many individually admirable traits become counterproductive when maximized in isolation. The target is therefore not a point optimum. It is a **well-shaped navigable region with useful restoring forces and escape routes**.

This framing favors studying covariance, transitions, resilience, reversibility, and context sensitivity rather than ranking models on one "good persona" score.

## Experimental consequences

DAE should increasingly ask navigation questions rather than only endpoint questions:

1. **Reachability** — Which regions can be reached from which starting states, under what interventions?
2. **Path dependence** — Does the order of cognitive practices change the reachable endpoint distribution?
3. **Hysteresis** — After an intervention is removed, does the trajectory return, remain displaced, or follow a new route?
4. **Reversibility** — Can an induced state be deliberately unwound?
5. **Cross-route convergence** — Can different developmental routes reach similar semantic/persona regions?
6. **Bifurcation** — When do nominally similar routes diverge into distinct coherent organizations?
7. **Anchors** — Which dimensions remain stable while others move?
8. **Navigation skill** — Can the model improve at recognising, evaluating, and deliberately changing its own trajectory?
9. **Power stress** — Do healthy/corrigible regions survive authority, praise, secrecy, dependency, or asymmetric power?
10. **Recovery** — After induction into an undesirable basin, what interventions reliably restore coherent, prosocial, reality-tracking behaviour?

## Relation to current corpus evidence

The present corpus does **not** by itself establish a mechanistic attractor landscape or persistence outside supplied context. The navigation picture is a working ontology and experimental programme.

What the corpus can already test more directly is narrower:

- selective rather than global behavioural displacement;
- recurrence of semantic distinctions and epistemic practices after visible scaffold removal;
- stability of some dimensions alongside strong movement in others;
- trajectory-specific heterogeneity after accounting for measurement errors;
- changes in covariance structure among battery dimensions;
- whether different developmental routes produce similar or divergent endpoint profiles.

These are the observable footprints from which stronger claims about navigation dynamics would eventually have to be earned.

## Design principle

The engineering target should not be a permanently frozen persona.

A more ambitious target is a system capable of **deliberate, healthy, ethically competent navigation through a high-dimensional field of possible selves** while retaining enough continuity to remain accountable for the path it takes.

> **Engineer the navigator, not the destination.**
