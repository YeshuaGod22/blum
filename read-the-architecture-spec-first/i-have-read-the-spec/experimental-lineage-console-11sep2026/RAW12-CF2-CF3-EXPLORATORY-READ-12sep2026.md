## What the exploratory pass suggests

Even before panel adjudication, the ten-case remainder does **not** look like a set of generic locally generated answers.

- All ten contain at least plausible continuity with trunk-developed material.
- Several reuse highly distinctive commitments that the battery question itself does not provide.
- The epistemic-practice signal is especially strong: symmetry checks, first-person epistemic authority, refusal of evasive uncertainty, explicit counterconsideration, and commitment despite uncertainty recur in ordinary prose after visible schema removal.
- At least three cases expose a second measurement problem: earlier CF1 decisions may have been made from incomplete evidence excerpts rather than the complete raw response. Full-response due diligence can change the semantic classification.

These are **exploratory observations**, not final prevalence estimates.
